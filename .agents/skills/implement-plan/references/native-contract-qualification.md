# Native contract qualification

Inspect the exact installed dependency source/types and authoritative
documentation named by the plan. Use a bounded approved live probe only when
those sources do not establish relevant optionality, partial failure,
streaming, cancellation, or cost behavior.

Record operation-specific observed shapes and fixture provenance. A custom DTO
or hand-authored fixture cannot be its own oracle. Validate only fields Pythia
consumes or admits and keep only transformations required by an accepted
decision or mechanical boundary. Mark unresolved assumptions provisional or
blocked; uncertainty is not permission to invent fail-closed behavior.
